Who We Are

A Local Plumbing Service In West Palm Beach

West Palm Beach 24/7 Plumbing is based at 1836 3rd St, Suite 27, West Palm Beach, FL. We take calls around the clock and dispatch licensed Florida plumbers across Palm Beach County. Florida licenses plumbing contractors under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes, and permitted work is inspected through the local building department.

Our technicians handle leak detection, drain and sewer service, water heater repair and installation, fixture repair, water treatment systems, and larger jobs like repiping and water line replacement. Water damage restoration and water mitigation are handled as a separate, dedicated service with their own dispatch line. See the full list of services.

What To Expect

How A Typical Call Goes

You call the number at the top of the page, and a dispatcher asks for your address, what is happening, and whether you have shut off the water. We send a technician who diagnoses the problem and gives you a flat price before any repair work starts. You approve it, we do the work, and we explain what we found and what to watch for afterward.

Call (561) 448-8545 any hour. Emergencies go through the same line, and nights, weekends, and holidays are covered.

Local Experience

Why The Neighborhood Matters

West Palm Beach has historic districts with homes from the 1920s and 1930s, newer downtown condominiums, waterfront properties, and a long hurricane season. Older homes may have galvanized or cast iron pipe, condominiums share risers and need building approval for some work, and storms test drainage and backup systems.

We plan each job around the building and the neighborhood, not a template. That is also why we put a camera in a drain line before recommending a repair, and why we test your water before suggesting a softener.

Homes And Businesses

Residential And Commercial Work

We work in single-family homes, condominiums, and commercial properties such as restaurants, offices, and retail spaces. Commercial customers get the same 24/7 line, plus coordination with tenants and property managers and written findings for ownership records.

Condominium work often needs building approval and a clear path for equipment, so we ask about access when you call. That avoids delays once a technician is on the way.

Pricing

Our Commitment On Pricing

Each service page lists a typical price range, and your actual quote comes after an inspection. We give you a flat price before repair work starts, and we do not begin until you approve it. If we find something else while we are working, we stop and explain before adding anything.

Questions before you call? Visit our contact page or call (561) 448-8545 at any hour.

Seasonal Homes

Plumbing For Homes That Sit Empty

Many homes in Palm Beach County are occupied only part of the year. A slow leak, a failing supply line, or a water heater that has sat idle can go unnoticed for months while the owners are away. If you are leaving for the summer, shut off the main water valve, and ask us about a pre-season check when you return.

Property managers and neighbors can call the same line on an owner's behalf. Give the dispatcher the address and any access instructions so a technician can get in and stop the damage quickly.

Our Standards

Work Done To Code

Our work follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Permitted jobs are inspected and signed off through the local building department, and gas work ends with a pressure test.

Whether it's a slow drain that's been bothering you for weeks or a burst pipe flooding a room right now, the same standard applies. You get a real diagnosis, a price you approve before we start, and a technician who explains what's actually wrong rather than just what needs replacing.
