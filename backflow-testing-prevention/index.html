Backflow Testing & Prevention in West Palm Beach, FL

Backflow prevention keeps contaminated water from siphoning back into the clean supply line, and Florida properties with irrigation systems or certain commercial connections are typically required to have devices tested annually. We handle both installation and the required certified testing. Missing a required test date can result in a compliance notice from your utility, so tracking the annual schedule matters as much as the test itself.

FAQs

Common Questions

Do I actually need backflow testing every year?

Most properties with irrigation systems or certain commercial connections in Florida are required to test annually, and your water utility can confirm your specific requirement.

What happens if my device fails the test?

We can typically repair or replace the failed component on the spot and retest before we leave.

Is this required for a simple residential sprinkler system?

In most cases, yes. Irrigation connections are a common trigger for backflow prevention requirements.

Who do I need to send the test results to?

We provide documentation in the format your specific water utility or jurisdiction requires for their records.

I got a notice from my water utility about a required annual test, what is that?

That notice is almost always about backflow preventer testing, required annually for properties with irrigation systems or certain other connections. We can confirm your device and get it tested to clear that notice.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Testing follows the certified procedure your water utility requires, checking that the device prevents reverse flow under simulated failure conditions, with results documented on the standard compliance form. New installations are sized to the connection type, whether residential irrigation or a larger commercial service line, and positioned per code requirements for accessibility and drainage in case the device needs to relieve pressure.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Irrigation systems are near-universal on West Palm Beach residential lots given the year-round growing season, which is exactly the kind of connection that typically triggers a backflow prevention requirement under Florida plumbing code. New landscaping or irrigation work on a property is often the first time a homeowner learns a device is required at all.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Most jurisdictions and utilities require this test annually, and we can flag your property for a reminder around the same time next year so you're not relying on remembering the exact date. If a device was repaired rather than replaced, it's worth having it re-tested at the standard interval rather than waiting longer since it's already shown one failure.

Test results are documented on the standard compliance form your utility or jurisdiction requires, and we'll confirm with you that the paperwork was filed correctly so you're not left chasing that down yourself.
