Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

West Palm Beach's downtown and Clematis Street corridor have a dense concentration of restaurants and retail spaces. Many are in older buildings with plumbing that predates current commercial code, which shapes a lot of the calls we get from property managers in that area. Older commercial buildings in that corridor also tend to share plumbing risers between units, which can complicate isolating an issue to a single tenant space.
