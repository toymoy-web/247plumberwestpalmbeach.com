FAQs

Common Questions

Will snaking damage my older pipes?

We choose the method based on pipe age and material. Older cast iron and clay lines are handled with more conservative equipment than newer PVC.

How do I know if I need hydro jetting instead of snaking?

If a line clogs again within weeks of snaking, that's usually a sign of grease buildup or root intrusion that hydro jetting clears more completely.

Can this wait until tomorrow?

A single slow drain can usually wait for a scheduled appointment, but multiple backed-up fixtures at once should be treated as urgent.

Can a clog in one bathroom affect other fixtures?

If it's isolated to a single fixture's trap, no, but a clog further down a shared line can affect multiple fixtures at once.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

For most clogs we start with mechanical cable snaking, which physically breaks up and retrieves the blockage. If a line clogs again shortly after, or if the buildup is grease or root intrusion rather than a single object, we recommend hydro jetting instead, which scours the full pipe interior rather than just clearing a path through it. A camera inspection is included when the cause of a recurring clog isn't obvious, so the fix matches the actual problem rather than repeating the same temporary clearing.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Mature tree canopy in older West Palm Beach neighborhoods means root intrusion is a genuinely common cause of recurring drain clogs here, not just an occasional edge case. We check for that pattern specifically before assuming a clog is a one-time blockage.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

After a line is cleared, we recommend avoiding grease disposal down kitchen drains and periodic hot water flushes to help keep buildup from returning as quickly. If hydro jetting was used because of grease or root buildup, an annual maintenance jetting can keep the line clear longer than waiting for the next full clog.

If a line we cleared backs up again within a short window under normal use, we'll come back out to reassess at no additional service call charge, since that usually points to something we should have caught the first time.
