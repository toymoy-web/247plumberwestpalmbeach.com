The Neighborhood

El Cid At A Glance

Where It Is

The El Cid Historic District is bounded by Flamingo Drive, South Flagler Drive, Dyer Road and South Dixie Highway. It sits near downtown West Palm Beach and the western shore of Lake Worth.

How It Began

El Cid was largely a product of the 1920s Florida land boom. Developer Jay Phipps assembled former pineapple fields and other property, and between 1923 and 1926 independent builders sold homes on most of the available lots.

The Homes

The houses are predominantly Spanish-style, mostly Mediterranean Revival and Mission Style. Each home was built by its own builder, so layouts and floor plans vary from lot to lot.

Historic Status

The district contains 281 historic buildings. It was added to the National Register of Historic Places on August 31, 1995, and is also listed on the local and state registers.

Plumbing Help

Plumbing Help For El Cid Homes

A neighborhood built block by block by separate builders means no two houses are guaranteed to share the same layout. That is why we start with a conversation about your house: how many bathrooms, where the water heater sits, and what changed recently. The more we know before arriving, the better prepared the truck is.

Many El Cid homes are close to a century old, and plenty have been renovated since. We do not assume what is inside the walls of any particular house. When a drain keeps slowing down, a sewer camera inspection shows the actual condition of the line, so you decide based on what is there rather than on a guess.

Historic homes can carry extra rules when the work changes how a house looks from the street. Interior repairs usually do not, but it is sensible to ask the City of West Palm Beach before any exterior change, such as a new vent or a relocated meter-side line.

General Guidance

General Tips For Older Homes

These are general suggestions, not findings about any one El Cid address. Find your main water shutoff before you need it, and test it once so you know it turns. If a toilet runs on its own, listen for it at night, because a quiet leak can waste a lot of water.

Read your water meter when no one is using water. If the dial still moves, water is escaping somewhere. That check costs nothing, and it gives you a clear fact to share when you call.

FAQs

Questions From El Cid

Do you serve all of the El Cid historic district?

Yes. We take calls from addresses throughout El Cid, from Flamingo Drive to Dyer Road. Give the dispatcher your street address when you call so they can confirm it.

Do I need city approval for plumbing work in a historic district?

Often not for interior repairs, but exterior changes may need review. Ask the City of West Palm Beach before starting, and we can describe what the plumbing work involves.

How do you find out what is wrong with an old drain?

We can run a camera through the line. It shows the real condition, such as roots, buildup or a break, so the repair plan matches the problem.

Should I replace all the pipes in my house?

Not automatically. Repeated leaks in the same system are a reason to discuss repiping, but a single repair does not mean the whole house needs it. We explain the options before any work.

What should I do while waiting for a plumber?

Shut off the main valve if water is flowing, turn off power to wet areas if it is safe, and move belongings away from the water. Then call (561) 448-8545.
