Common Signs

When To Call Us

Constant Dripping

Almost always a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring inside the faucet body.

Leak Under The Sink

Usually a failed supply line, shutoff valve, or P-trap connection.

Low Water Pressure At One Faucet

Can point to a clogged aerator or a partially closed shutoff valve.

Handle Loose Or Hard To Turn

Often a sign the internal cartridge is wearing out.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing or should I replace it?

If the fixture itself is in good shape, a cartridge or washer repair usually solves it for far less than a full replacement.

What's causing the leak I see under my cabinet?

Most under-sink leaks trace to a failed supply line connection or a corroded shutoff valve rather than the faucet itself.

Can you match my existing faucet finish if I only need part replaced?

Yes, repairs typically reuse your existing fixture, so the finish stays consistent.

What if my faucet brand is no longer manufactured?

We can often source compatible aftermarket cartridges, or recommend a replacement fixture if parts genuinely aren't available.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Faucet repairs typically involve cartridge or valve stem replacement matched to your specific fixture's internal mechanism, since ceramic disc, compression, and ball-valve faucets each use different repair parts. Under-sink work includes inspecting and replacing supply lines, shutoff valves, and P-trap connections as needed. We carry a range of common cartridges and valves on the truck to avoid unnecessary return trips.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

South Florida's humidity and salt air can accelerate corrosion on exposed metal fixtures and under-sink shutoff valves faster than in drier inland climates. That is part of why a valve that looks fine can still be seized or corroded internally.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Faucet And Sink Repairs In Historic Homes

Faucet repair is where historic charm meets practical limits. Many homes in Flamingo Park, Mango Promenade, and Northwood keep original or period-style fixtures that owners want to preserve, so we try to repair the cartridge or valve before suggesting replacement. In waterfront buildings near Flagler Drive, salt air corrodes exposed supply lines and shutoff valves under sinks faster than it does inland, which is worth checking on any faucet visit.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

After a cartridge or valve replacement, the fixture should perform like new with no special maintenance required. If you replaced a full fixture, we'll note the model for you in case you ever need a matching part in the future, since manufacturers do phase out specific finishes and models over time.

Repairs are pressure-tested under normal water flow before we consider the job finished, checking both the repaired point and nearby connections that could be affected by the same water pressure.
