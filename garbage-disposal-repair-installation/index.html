Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation in West Palm Beach, FL

Written by the West Palm Beach 24/7 Plumbing team. Last updated October 2026.

A disposal that hums but doesn't spin is usually jammed, not dead, and a leak at the base often means a worn seal rather than a failed unit. We troubleshoot both before recommending replacement, and install new units when repair genuinely isn't the better option. Most disposals have a rated service life of several years under normal household use, so an early failure is worth diagnosing rather than assuming the unit is simply worn out.

FAQs

Common Questions

My disposal just hums, is it broken?

Not necessarily. That's usually a jam, and clearing it with the reset button and the hex wrench slot often solves it without replacement.

Why is water leaking from under my disposal?

That's typically the mounting seal wearing out over time, which is a repair, not a sign the whole unit needs replacing.

How long does a new disposal installation take?

Most installations, including reconnecting the dishwasher line, are completed within an hour or two.

Can you install a higher-horsepower unit than what I have now?

Yes, we can upgrade horsepower during installation as long as your electrical setup supports it.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Most jams are cleared using the disposal's built-in reset button combined with manually rotating the flywheel from underneath using the hex wrench slot most units include. If a leak is found, we identify whether it's the mounting seal, the dishwasher inlet connection, or the discharge pipe connection before repairing, since each has a different fix. New installations include reconnecting the dishwasher drain line and testing the full system before we finish.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Coastal humidity in West Palm Beach can accelerate corrosion on a disposal's mounting hardware over time. That is one reason base leaks around older units here show up sooner than the unit's rated lifespan would suggest.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Disposal Problems From Kitchen To Kitchen

Disposals fail faster where kitchens see heavy use, and West Palm Beach has both extremes. Restaurant kitchens along Clematis Street and in Northwood Village push commercial-style loads through their drains, while older homes in Grandview Heights and El Cid run small residential units on original drain lines that do not forgive grease. When a disposal jams repeatedly, we check the line downstream before blaming the unit.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

After a repair or new installation, running cold water for a few seconds before and after each use helps flush debris through and reduces the chance of a repeat jam. New units typically don't need attention beyond normal use for years, but a leak at the seal is worth catching early if it ever starts.

New installations and repairs are run through a full test cycle with water flowing before we leave, confirming there's no leak at any connection point under normal use.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

Garbage disposal leaks at the drain connection sometimes point to a broader kitchen drain issue, which drain cleaning covers. If you're replacing the disposal as part of a larger kitchen plumbing update, faucet and sink repair is the other fixture most commonly serviced at the same time.
