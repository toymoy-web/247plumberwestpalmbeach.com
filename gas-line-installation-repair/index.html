Scope Of Service

What's Included

Every gas line job, whether a new appliance run or a repair, ends with a pressure test using a manometer. It confirms there's no measurable pressure drop over time, which is the definitive way to confirm there's no leak anywhere in the line. New installations are sized to the appliance's BTU demand rather than just matched to the existing line diameter, since undersizing is a common cause of appliances not getting enough gas pressure to perform properly.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Outdoor kitchens and gas-fired pool heaters are common additions in this market given the climate, and each one requires a properly sized, code-compliant gas line run rather than tapping into an existing line undersized for the new load.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Gas Work In Historic Districts And Downtown

Gas work comes up when owners in historic districts like El Cid and Flamingo Park add a range, a tankless water heater, or an outdoor kitchen to a home built before those loads existed. It also comes up when downtown restaurants add equipment. Each addition needs the line sized to the appliance, a permit, and a pressure test, and in a historic district the route has to be planned around the house instead of through it.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Once a line passes its pressure test, no ongoing maintenance is typically needed for the line itself, though any new gas appliance should follow its own manufacturer's service schedule. If you ever smell gas again after this work, even faintly, that's always worth an immediate call regardless of how recently the line was serviced.

No gas line job is considered complete without a documented pressure test showing no measurable drop over time. That is the standard we hold every installation and repair to regardless of job size.

Codes And Standards

Work Done To Code

Gas piping work falls under the Florida Building Code, Fuel Gas, which is based on the International Fuel Gas Code (IFGC). Permits and inspections go through the local building department, and the final pressure test is the standard way to verify a line before gas is restored.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

New gas appliances are often installed alongside other kitchen or outdoor plumbing work, and water heater installation is the most common pairing when a gas-fired unit is involved. Commercial properties adding gas service typically need this coordinated with broader commercial plumbing work.
