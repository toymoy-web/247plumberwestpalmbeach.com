The Neighborhood

Grandview Heights At A Glance

Where It Is

The district is bounded by Park Place, Alabama Avenue, M Street and Lake Avenue. The neighborhood association describes it as sitting on the Atlantic Ridge, just south of Okeechobee Boulevard.

How It Began

The neighborhood was established in 1910 and platted as three subdivisions in the 1910s and 1920s. It was one of the first speculative suburbs, built for working and middle-class residents, and most historic homes were finished by 1925.

The Homes

Most houses are bungalows, mainly Craftsman and Mission styles, with some Mediterranean Revival homes. The district contains 268 historic buildings.

Historic Status

Grandview Heights was listed on the National Register of Historic Places on July 8, 1999. It is one of 13 historic districts in West Palm Beach.

Plumbing Help

Plumbing For Bungalow-Scale Homes

Bungalows are modest in size, so space is tight. Water heaters, shutoff valves and cleanouts may be in small closets or utility corners. When you call, tell us where the water heater is and roughly how old it is, and we can plan for the space.

A water heater repair is often the quick fix when hot water fades. If the unit is leaking or has failed completely, replacement may be the safer option, and we explain the choice instead of pushing one answer.

Hidden leaks can sit behind walls in any house. Leak detection uses tools to locate the source, so a small area is opened instead of a large one. If a supply line has failed, water line repair is the next step.

General Guidance

Checks Any Homeowner Can Do

These tips apply to any older or smaller home and say nothing specific about your address. Look under sinks and around the water heater for moisture, rust or a musty smell. Small drips are easier and cheaper to fix than a long-running leak.

If your water bill rises without a change in habits, compare readings on your meter over a few hours when no one is home. A moving dial points to a leak somewhere between the meter and your fixtures.

FAQs

Questions From Grandview Heights

Do you work on Craftsman bungalows?

We work on homes of all ages. Tell us what is happening and where the water heater and shutoffs are, and we will plan the visit around the house.

My hot water is lukewarm. Is the water heater done?

Not always. A failed element, thermostat or burner can cause it. We diagnose the unit first and tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense.

Can a leak be found without cutting into walls?

Often yes. Electronic leak detection narrows the location so that any opening is small. Not every leak can be traced this way, and we say so when that is the case.

Can you help with a bungalow renovation?

Yes, for the plumbing side. Tell us what is being changed, such as a bathroom or kitchen, and we can explain what the plumbing work involves before you commit.

Is the neighborhood association involved in plumbing work?

Not for ordinary repairs. If your project changes the outside of a historic home, ask the City of West Palm Beach what review applies.
