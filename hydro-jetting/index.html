FAQs

Common Questions

Why did my clog come back after snaking?

Snaking often just punches through a clog rather than removing it. If grease or roots caused it, they're usually still coating the pipe walls, which is exactly what jetting is built to remove.

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

Yes, when pressure is adjusted appropriately for the pipe's age and material, which we assess with a camera first.

How long does jetting keep a line clear?

Since it removes buildup rather than just clearing a path, jetted lines typically stay clear significantly longer than snaked ones.

Is jetting more expensive than snaking?

Generally yes upfront, but because it clears buildup rather than just opening a path, it often costs less over time by preventing repeat service calls.

The plumber said they couldn't snake it all the way through, what now?

That usually means the snake hit root intrusion or heavy buildup it couldn't push past. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water instead of a cable, which clears that kind of blockage far more reliably.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Jetting uses a specialized nozzle that sends high-pressure water both forward and backward as it's pulled through the line. It scours grease, scale, and root intrusion off the full interior pipe wall rather than just punching through the center of a clog. Pressure is calibrated to the pipe's age and material. Older or more fragile pipe is jetted at a lower, safer pressure than modern PVC. A camera pass beforehand confirms jetting is the appropriate method for the specific pipe condition found.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Lines that were jetted due to grease buildup benefit from an annual maintenance jetting to stay ahead of the problem before it returns to clog-causing levels. Lines jetted for root intrusion are more likely to need monitoring via a follow-up camera check, since the roots themselves can regrow at the same entry point over time.

A follow-up camera pass after jetting confirms the line is genuinely clear along its full length, not just flowing again at the moment we finish.
