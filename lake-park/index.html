What Makes Lake Park Different

Why Lake Park Plumbing Is Its Own Market

A Planned Town From The 1920s

The town was founded in 1923 as Kelsey City and renamed Lake Park in 1939. It was laid out by Dr. John Nolan with the Olmsted Brothers and was the first zoned municipality in Florida, so older streets and buildings date back a century.

Historic Buildings On Park Avenue

Lake Park Town Hall was built in 1927 and is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Older commercial and civic buildings often have supply and drain lines that predate current code, which shapes how we plan a repair.

Waterfront On The Intracoastal

The eastern edge of the town borders the Intracoastal Waterway, with views toward Singer Island. Low elevation and nearby water make leak tracing, drainage, and seawall-adjacent plumbing part of everyday calls.

Homes And Small Businesses Side By Side

Lake Park has about 3,800 housing units, many close to restaurants and shops along Park Avenue. A drain problem in one building can involve a shared line, so we confirm what is yours before we start.

Local Overview

Plumbing In Lake Park

Coverage

Lake Park is a town of about 9,000 residents between Riviera Beach and North Palm Beach, a few miles north of West Palm Beach. The 2020 census counted 9,047 residents and about 3,755 housing units.

Background

The town began as Kelsey City, named for Boston entrepreneur Harry Seymour Kelsey, who bought 30,000 acres north of West Palm Beach. Its 1927 Town Hall survived the 1928 Okeechobee hurricane and sheltered residents, and the town's website points residents to Seacoast Utility Authority for water quality reports.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4

Call our 24/7 line, a Lake Park technician can be dispatched immediately, day or night.
