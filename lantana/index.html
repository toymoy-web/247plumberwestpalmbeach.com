What Makes Lantana Different

Why Lantana Plumbing Is Its Own Market

Postwar Homes And Older Cores

Settlers arrived in the 1800s, the town incorporated in 1921, and a building boom followed World War II. That mix means original drain lines, galvanized supply pipe, and aging water heaters show up on many service calls.

A Town With A Lot Of Water

About a fifth of the town's total area is water, so many properties sit close to the Intracoastal and nearby canals. Moisture, corrosion, and drainage problems tend to show up sooner on those lots.

I-95 And The Commercial Strip

Interstate 95 was completed through Lantana in the mid-1970s and brought commercial development along its corridor. Shops, offices, and restaurants near the highway have their own plumbing needs and after-hours emergencies.

Between Lake Worth Beach And Boynton Beach

Lantana sits between two of our larger service areas, so technicians already run routes through town. That keeps dispatch practical even for a small town of about 11,500 residents.

Local Overview

Plumbing In Lantana

Coverage

Lantana is a town of about 11,500 residents in southern Palm Beach County, roughly nine to ten miles south of West Palm Beach. It covers 2.37 square miles of land and counted about 5,700 housing units in the 2020 census.

Background

The Town of Lantana was incorporated on July 20, 1921, and takes its name from the wild lantana plants that once grew there. Oyster gathering was a leading industry in the early 1900s, and postwar growth plus the arrival of I-95 shaped the town's current mix of homes and businesses.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4

Call our 24/7 line, a Lantana technician can be dispatched immediately, day or night.
