Common Signs

When To Call Us

Rising Water Bill

A bill that climbs with no change in usage is the most common leak signal we see.

Meter Spinning With Everything Off

If the dial moves with every fixture shut, water is escaping somewhere in the line.

Warm Spot On The Floor

A warm patch usually means a hot water line leaking under the slab.

Sound Of Running Water

A hissing or trickling sound behind a wall with no fixture running is worth checking immediately.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you find a leak without cutting into my floor?

Yes, in most cases. Acoustic and thermal tracing locates the source first, so cutting is targeted, not exploratory.

What if the leak is in my pool line, not the house?

We check both. Pool equipment pads and buried pool plumbing are a common source of unexplained water loss in this area.

How fast can someone come out?

Same-day appointments are available for most calls, and our emergency line runs 24/7 for active leaks.

Do you check outdoor and irrigation lines too?

Yes, buried yard lines and irrigation systems are checked as part of a full leak detection visit, not just the plumbing inside the house.

My meter keeps spinning even with everything off, what does that mean?

It means water is escaping somewhere between the meter and your fixtures. That's the single clearest sign of a leak, and it's exactly what our leak detection equipment is used to trace to an exact location.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Our detection equipment includes acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of water escaping under pressure, and thermal imaging that spots temperature differences where hot water lines are leaking. For pool systems, we also pressure-test the equipment pad and buried lines separately from the household plumbing, since a pool leak and a house leak require different isolation steps. This visit covers locating the leak; the repair itself, whether it's a slab leak, a yard line, or an indoor fixture, is quoted separately once we know exactly what we're dealing with.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

West Palm Beach's rainy season and high water table make it harder to spot a slab or yard leak by eye, since ground moisture is common anyway. That's part of why we lean on electronic tracing rather than visual inspection alone, especially in neighborhoods like El Cid and Flamingo Park where original 1920s-era plumbing sits under newer landscaping.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Once a leak is located and repaired, we recommend a follow-up meter check about a week later to confirm the fix fully resolved the water loss, since a slow secondary leak can sometimes hide behind a larger one. If the leak was in an older section of pipe, it's worth asking whether nearby sections show similar wear before another leak surfaces in the same run.

We stand behind our leak location work: if a repair is made based on our diagnosis and the original symptom doesn't resolve, we'll come back and re-diagnose at no additional charge for the visit itself.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

If a leak turns out to be under the slab rather than in an accessible line, slab leak detection and repair is the more targeted next step, and if the source is an aging service line from the street, water line repair covers that separately. When a leak has already caused standing water or damaged flooring, water damage restoration handles the cleanup once the source is fixed.
