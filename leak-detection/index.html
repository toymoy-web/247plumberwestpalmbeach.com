24/7 Emergency

Emergency Leak Detection In West Palm Beach, Day Or Night

Some leaks cannot wait for business hours. A meter that spins with every fixture off, water stains spreading across a ceiling, or a wet spot growing on the floor all call for an emergency response. Our dispatch line is open 24/7, and we send a technician to find the source before the damage spreads.

If you can, shut off the main valve while you wait and note your meter reading. Those two steps slow the damage and give us a starting point for tracing the leak, whether it's in a wall, under the slab, or in a buried line.

Common Signs

When To Call Us

Rising Water Bill

A bill that climbs with no change in usage is the most common leak signal we see.

Meter Spinning With Everything Off

If the dial moves with every fixture shut, water is escaping somewhere in the line.

Warm Spot On The Floor

A warm patch usually means a hot water line leaking under the slab.

Sound Of Running Water

A hissing or trickling sound behind a wall with no fixture running is worth checking immediately.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can you find a leak without cutting into my floor?

Yes, in most cases. Acoustic and thermal tracing locates the source first, so cutting is targeted, not exploratory.

What if the leak is in my pool line, not the house?

We check both. Pool equipment pads and buried pool plumbing are a common source of unexplained water loss in this area.

How fast can someone come out?

Same-day appointments are available for most calls, and our emergency line runs 24/7 for active leaks.

Do you check outdoor and irrigation lines too?

Yes, buried yard lines and irrigation systems are checked as part of a full leak detection visit, not just the plumbing inside the house.

My meter keeps spinning even with everything off, what does that mean?

It means water is escaping somewhere between the meter and your fixtures. That's the single clearest sign of a leak, and it's exactly what our leak detection equipment is used to trace to an exact location.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Our detection equipment includes acoustic listening devices that pick up the sound of water escaping under pressure, and thermal imaging that spots temperature differences where hot water lines are leaking. For pool systems, we also pressure-test the equipment pad and buried lines separately from the household plumbing, since a pool leak and a house leak require different isolation steps.

This visit covers locating the leak. The repair itself, whether it's a slab leak, a yard line, or an indoor fixture, is quoted separately once we know exactly what we're dealing with.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

West Palm Beach's rainy season and high water table make it harder to spot a slab or yard leak by eye, since ground moisture is common anyway. That's part of why we lean on electronic tracing rather than visual inspection alone, especially in neighborhoods like El Cid and Flamingo Park where original 1920s-era plumbing sits under newer landscaping.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Leak Detection Calls Across West Palm Beach

Leak calls cluster where the housing is oldest and the ground is wettest, including El Cid, Flamingo Park, and Mango Promenade south of downtown, and the 1920s cottages of Grandview Heights and Old Northwood. Properties along Flagler Drive and the Lake Worth Lagoon add a second wrinkle, because ground that stays wet most of the year can hide a small leak that would show up quickly elsewhere. Electronic tracing lets us separate a true leak from groundwater before anyone opens a wall.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Once a leak is located and repaired, we recommend a follow-up meter check about a week later to confirm the fix fully resolved the water loss, since a slow secondary leak can sometimes hide behind a larger one. If the leak was in an older section of pipe, it's worth asking whether nearby sections show similar wear before another leak surfaces in the same run.

We stand behind our leak location work: if a repair is made based on our diagnosis and the original symptom doesn't resolve, we'll come back and re-diagnose at no additional charge for the visit itself.

Codes And Standards

Work Done To Code

West Palm Beach's city code, Section 90-12, addresses billing adjustments for qualifying leaks on your side of the meter, and it requires a certified plumber to complete the repair within seven days. Keep your written findings and repair invoice, since the utility may ask for documentation. Repairs follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).
