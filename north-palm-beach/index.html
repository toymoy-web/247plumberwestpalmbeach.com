What Makes North Palm Beach Different

Why North Palm Beach Plumbing Is Its Own Market

Waterfront And Canal Lots

Much of the village was built on dredged former mangrove swamp and farmland, with waterfront cul-de-sacs cut out of the land. Elevation is only about seven feet, so drainage, irrigation, and supply lines all work close to the water table.

Homes From The 1950s And 1960s

The village incorporated in 1956 and was recognized that year as a planned community, so many homes are now close to seventy years old. Homes from that era often have original cast iron drain lines or older supply piping, which is why we put a camera in the line before recommending a repair.

An Island Village

North Palm Beach sits on an island surrounded by the Intracoastal Waterway and is reached by three bridges. Our dispatchers plan routes around bridge openings and traffic on U.S. 1 and Northlake Boulevard.

Long-Time And Seasonal Residents

The median age here is above 55, and a large share of residents are 65 or older, so many homeowners want a technician who explains each step and quotes the price first. Some homes also sit vacant for part of the year, which lets slow leaks run unnoticed.

Local Overview

Plumbing In North Palm Beach

Coverage

North Palm Beach is a village of about 13,100 residents in northern Palm Beach County, roughly eight miles north of West Palm Beach by road. It incorporated on August 13, 1956, and the 2020 census counted about 8,000 housing units.

Background

In 1954, John D. MacArthur bought 2,600 acres that included most of present-day North Palm Beach, Lake Park, Palm Beach Gardens, and Palm Beach Shores. North Palm Beach grew from that tract as one of the county's early planned communities, and its public golf course opened in 1963.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4
