The Neighborhood

Old Northwood At A Glance

Where It Is

The district is bounded by Broadway, North Dixie Highway, and 26th and 35th Streets in West Palm Beach.

How It Began

In the 1920s land boom, the Pinewood Development Company, led by David F. Dunkle, bought 400 acres here. More than 50 individual builders each built at least one house, which gave the neighborhood its varied look.

The Homes

Homes are Frame Vernacular, Mission style and Mediterranean. A network of alleys that give access to garages and services is one of the district's most distinctive features.

Historic Status

Old Northwood became West Palm Beach's first designated historic district in 1991, and it was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1994. The neighborhood association formed in 1987.

Plumbing Help

Homes And Businesses In One Corner Of Town

Northwood Road is the historic main street of Northwood Village, about a mile from downtown according to the Palm Beaches tourism site. It is lined with locally owned shops, cafes and galleries. That mix means we may be asked to help with a home water heater in the morning and a business drain in the afternoon.

For businesses, commercial plumbing covers restrooms, kitchens and water heating. Slow floor drains and grease-related blockages are common reasons that small food businesses call, and hydro jetting can clear a line more thoroughly than a standard snake.

At home, the alleys behind many Old Northwood houses mean service access can differ from a typical street-front layout. We ask where your cleanout and water heater are so we know where to start.

General Guidance

Tips For Small Businesses

This is general guidance for any food or retail business. Train staff to scrape plates before washing, keep grease out of drains, and know where the main shutoff is. A short written plan for who to call saves time when a drain backs up during service.

Schedule routine drain maintenance before a busy season rather than after a backup. Ask your landlord who is responsible for the building drain line versus the tenant space, because that affects who calls us.

FAQs

Questions From Old Northwood

Do you serve both homes and businesses in Northwood?

Yes. We handle residential calls in Old Northwood and commercial calls in Northwood Village. Tell us which when you call so we can send the right equipment.

Is Northwood Village part of Old Northwood?

They are neighbors with different roles. The historic district is bounded by Broadway, North Dixie Highway, and 26th and 35th Streets, while Northwood Village is the shopping street nearby.

Can you clear a restaurant drain?

We can clear and jet many commercial drain lines. A recurring clog is a sign to ask about maintenance, and we explain what we find.

What if my water heater fails on a weekend?

Call (561) 448-8545 and describe what you see. We will tell you what to do first, such as turning off the supply if it is leaking.

Do alleys change how you work?

They can. Alley access may make it easier to reach some lines. We ask about layout so the visit goes smoothly.
