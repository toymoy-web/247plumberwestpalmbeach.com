City Overview

Plumbing Across Palm Beach Gardens

Coverage

Palm Beach Gardens was planned by developer John D. MacArthur starting in 1959 as a garden city and now holds about 59,000 residents. It is the home of PGA National Resort and a long list of gated golf communities such as Mirasol, BallenIsles, Frenchman's Creek, and Old Palm. The Gardens Mall, Downtown at the Gardens, and Legacy Place anchor the retail core.

Much of the housing dates from the 1970s through today, so plumbing calls here tend to come from aging water heaters, irrigation tie-ins, and pool equipment rather than century-old pipe.

What Palm Beach Gardens Homes Ask Us About

Gated Golf Communities

PGA National alone covers about 2,340 acres and more than 40 communities, and Mirasol, BallenIsles, and Frenchman's Creek add more. Gate access and HOA rules shape every dispatch, so we sort out entry before a technician leaves.

Irrigation And Backflow Demands

Landscaped, golf-adjacent neighborhoods run large irrigation systems, which means backflow devices that need annual testing and sprinkler tie-ins that can leak underground for weeks before anyone notices.

Pools And Lakefront Lots

A large share of Gardens homes have pools, screened lanais, or lake views. Pool equipment lines and buried supply lines are common sources of unexplained water loss, and one leak detection visit can check both.

Newer Homes, Same Sediment Rules

Housing here is younger than in West Palm Beach proper, but sediment still settles in any tank water heater. Newer tank and tankless units still need regular maintenance to reach their rated lifespan.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4

Call our 24/7 line, a Palm Beach Gardens technician can be dispatched immediately, day or night.

Our Commitment

Serving Palm Beach Gardens
