Town Overview

Plumbing On Palm Beach Island

Coverage

The Town of Palm Beach was incorporated on April 17, 1911. It sits on a barrier island between the Lake Worth Lagoon and the Atlantic, reached from West Palm Beach over a handful of bridges across the Intracoastal Waterway.

About 9,245 people live there year-round, and the island is known for Worth Avenue, Royal Poinciana Way, the Flagler Museum at Whitehall, and the Mediterranean Revival estates designed by Addison Mizner between 1919 and 1924. Housing mixes historic homes, large estates, and oceanfront condominium buildings, which makes plumbing here less about volume and more about older materials, high-value finishes, and salt air.

What Island Properties Ask Us About

Historic And Estate-Scale Homes

Houses from the 1920s onward often keep original piping behind plaster, stone, and custom millwork. Non-invasive leak tracing matters more here than anywhere else we work.

Salt Air And Waterfront Exposure

With the Atlantic on one side and the Intracoastal on the other, exposed fixtures, outdoor connections, and irrigation components corrode faster than they would inland.

Large Properties, Pools, And Irrigation

Big lots usually mean pools, fountains, guest quarters, and long buried lines, so a single water bill spike can have several possible sources that need to be isolated one by one.

Oceanfront Condominium Buildings

Condominium towers along South Ocean Boulevard bring shared risers and building-management rules, so we confirm whether a leak is inside a unit or in a common line before any work is scheduled.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4

Call our 24/7 line, a Palm Beach technician can be dispatched immediately, day or night.
