The Neighborhood

Prospect Park At A Glance

Where It Is

The Prospect Park-Southland Park Historic District is bounded by Lake Worth, South Dixie Highway, Monceaux Road and Monroe Drive. It sits in the southern part of West Palm Beach.

Era

The district includes buildings from 1922 to 1945. The city lists Prospect Park as one of its local historic districts, developed between 1920 and 1935.

The Homes

Prospect Park homes are Mediterranean Revival and Mission Revival, according to the city history summarized on Wikipedia.

Historic Status

The combined district was added to the National Register of Historic Places on April 8, 2011.

Plumbing Help

Plumbing Near Lake Worth

Because the district runs along Lake Worth, some homes are close to the water. We do not claim any flooding or drainage problem for a specific address. If you have seen standing water after heavy rain, tell us, and we will look at where it is entering and what plumbing is involved.

A building period from 1922 to 1945 covers more than two decades of changing construction. Many houses have been remodeled at some point, so a bathroom or kitchen may be newer than the rest of the house. Knowing what was updated helps us diagnose a problem faster.

Typical calls here include a failing water heater, a toilet that will not stop running, and a leak that has shown up as a stain or a bill. A water heater replacement is easiest to plan before the old unit leaks.

If you plan a remodel, bring up the plumbing early. Moving a sink, shower or toilet changes drain and supply routes, and it is easier to plan that before walls are closed. We can describe what a layout change involves, and you can confirm any permit requirement with the city before work starts.

General Guidance

If Water Gets Inside

This is general advice for any home. If a pipe bursts, shut off the main valve first, then turn off power to affected areas if it is safe. Move belongings and take photos for your records before cleanup starts.

Dry wet materials quickly. Standing moisture can lead to mold, so do not wait to start drying. Call and describe how far the water has spread.

FAQs

Questions From Prospect Park

Do you serve Southland Park as well as Prospect Park?

Yes. They are combined in one historic district bounded by Lake Worth, South Dixie Highway, Monceaux Road and Monroe Drive, and we serve addresses across it.

Do you inspect for flood risk?

No. We do not assess flood zones. If you are concerned, ask the City of West Palm Beach or Palm Beach County. We can help with the plumbing side of a water problem.

How do I know if I have a hidden leak?

Check your water meter when nothing is running. A moving dial, a rising bill or a damp spot all suggest a leak, and leak detection can locate it.

Is a running toilet worth fixing?

Yes. A running toilet can waste water and raise your bill, and it is usually a straightforward repair.

What should I do after a pipe bursts?

Shut off the main valve, cut power to wet areas if it is safe, and call. Drying should begin quickly after the water is stopped.
