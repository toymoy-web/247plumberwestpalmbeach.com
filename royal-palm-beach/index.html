What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4

Call our 24/7 line, a Royal Palm Beach technician can be dispatched immediately, day or night.

Our Commitment

Serving Royal Palm Beach

Because Royal Palm Beach grew through a series of planned developments rather than organically over a century, entire streets here often share the same builder, the same pipe material, and the same installation year. That's genuinely useful information on a service call: if one home on a block just had a water heater fail from age, it's worth checking whether neighboring homes from the same development phase are approaching the same point.

We keep that pattern in mind whether the call is a single-family home, a townhome, or a property inside one of the village's many HOA-managed communities. It's also why we ask which development or community a property is in during scheduling, since it often tells us something useful before a technician even arrives.
