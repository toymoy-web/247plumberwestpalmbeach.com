FAQs

Common Questions

How often does a septic tank actually need pumping?

Most residential tanks need pumping every 3-5 years, depending on household size and usage, though signs like slow drains can mean it's needed sooner.

What happens if I keep putting off pumping?

An overdue tank risks backing up into the house or damaging the drain field, which is a far more expensive repair than routine pumping.

Do you also inspect the drain field, or just pump the tank?

We check drain field function as part of the visit, since a struggling drain field is often the real issue behind repeat problems.

Can you tell if my drain field is failing versus just the tank being full?

Yes, our inspection during pumping checks drain field function specifically, since that distinction changes what repair, if any, is actually needed.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Pumping removes both the liquid and solid waste layers from the tank using a vacuum truck, and we inspect the tank's baffles and the general condition of the drain field's surface (looking for pooling, unusually lush grass, or odor) as part of the same visit. If the drain field itself shows signs of failure rather than the tank simply being full, that's a separate, more involved repair we'll explain clearly rather than folding it into a routine pumping invoice.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Unincorporated pockets of Palm Beach County west of the city, along with some older established neighborhoods never annexed into municipal sewer service, still rely entirely on septic systems, and the region's high water table can affect drain field performance more than it would in drier soil conditions.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Most residential tanks need pumping again in three to five years depending on household size and usage, and we can note your property for a reminder around that window. Between pumpings, being mindful of what goes down the drains, avoiding excess grease, chemicals, and non-biodegradable items, meaningfully extends how long the system performs well.

We document the tank's condition and drain field observations at every pumping visit, giving you a record you can reference if you ever need to show a home buyer or inspector the system's maintenance history.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

Properties on septic sometimes also deal with drain cleaning issues inside the house that are unrelated to the tank itself, and it's worth ruling that out separately. If a backup has already caused water damage indoors, water damage restoration handles the cleanup.
