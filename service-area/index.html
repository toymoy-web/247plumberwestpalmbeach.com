How Coverage Is Organized

Four Directions From Downtown

North of downtown we cover Riviera Beach, Lake Park, North Palm Beach, and Palm Beach Gardens. East across the Intracoastal is the island town of Palm Beach. South of downtown the route runs through Lake Worth Beach, Palm Springs, Lantana, and Boynton Beach, with Greenacres inland to the southwest.

West of downtown are Royal Palm Beach and Wellington, and farther northwest is The Acreage, where most homes use wells and septic systems.

Coverage Notes

What Is Different In Each Area

Each community has its own plumbing profile. Palm Beach is an island town reached by bridges from the mainland, so dispatch plans around bridge traffic. Wellington's equestrian properties add barn lines and large irrigation systems, and The Acreage is farther out and mostly on wells and septic. Older towns such as Lake Park and Lake Worth Beach have a century of housing stock, while Palm Beach Gardens and Royal Palm Beach have newer subdivisions.

Condominiums, gated communities, and commercial buildings often have their own access rules. Tell the dispatcher about gates, building management, or tenant coordination so the technician arrives ready to get in and work.

Around The Clock

Emergency Coverage In Every Community

Our emergency line covers every community listed on this page, 24/7, including nights, weekends, and holidays. For a burst pipe or an active leak, shut off the main water valve if you can reach it, cut power to wet areas at the breaker if it is safe, and call.

Many homes here are occupied only part of the year, so a slow leak can run for weeks before anyone notices. If you manage a seasonal property, give the dispatcher your access instructions so a technician can get in and stop the water without delay.

Dispatch

How Dispatch Works

Call (561) 448-8545 and tell the dispatcher your address, what is happening, and the type of building. We confirm that the address is in range, quote any travel charge before sending a technician, and give you the next available window. Emergency calls are answered 24/7, and standard calls get same-day windows.

Having a few details ready speeds things up: the nearest cross street, any gate code or access instructions, whether you have shut off the water, and roughly how old the home or building is. For condominiums and commercial buildings, mention whether building management needs to approve access.
