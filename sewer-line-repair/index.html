FAQs

Common Questions

Do you always recommend full replacement?

No. A camera inspection often shows the damage is localized enough for a spot repair or trenchless lining, which costs significantly less.

Why do my drains keep backing up in the same spot?

In older neighborhoods here, that's frequently root intrusion at a cracked joint that keeps regrowing after simple clearing.

Is a permit required for this repair in West Palm Beach?

Yes, sewer line repair work falls under Florida Building Code and requires city inspection sign-off, which we handle as part of the job.

How long does a typical sewer line repair take?

A spot repair is often completed in a single day; a full replacement can take one to three days depending on access and length.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Repair options range from a localized spot repair, where only the damaged section is excavated and replaced or lined, to a full trenchless pipe lining that rehabilitates the entire run without digging it up, to full excavation and replacement for lines with extensive, distributed damage. Which option applies is determined by camera inspection findings, not assumed upfront. Permit filing and the required city inspection are included as part of any sewer line repair job in West Palm Beach, performed to the Florida Building Code, Plumbing (based on the International Plumbing Code, or IPC).

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Palm Beach County's older residential blocks were largely built with clay and cast iron sewer lines that are now decades past their original service life, and the region's large, established trees make root intrusion at pipe joints a frequent, real finding rather than a rare one.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

After a spot repair or trenchless lining, we recommend a follow-up camera pass at the one-year mark, particularly for lines with a history of root intrusion, since it lets us catch any regrowth at the repair joint early rather than waiting for another backup. Full replacements generally don't need this same monitoring.

Repairs are camera-verified before we consider the job complete, and we'll provide the before-and-after footage so you can see the actual condition of the line, not just take our word for it.
