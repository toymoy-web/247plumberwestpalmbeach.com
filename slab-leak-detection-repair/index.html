FAQs

Common Questions

Do you have to break up my whole floor to find the leak?

No. Electronic tracing narrows the leak to a specific spot first, so only that area is opened.

Can the leak be rerouted instead of repaired in the slab?

Often, yes. Rerouting the line above the slab avoids breaking concrete entirely, and we'll quote both options.

How urgent is a slab leak?

It's not usually an immediate emergency like a burst pipe, but the longer it runs, the more water and potential foundation exposure builds up, so early diagnosis matters.

Will insurance cover a slab leak repair?

Many homeowner policies cover the cost to access and repair the leak itself. Coverage specifics vary by policy and are worth confirming with your insurer.

Why is one spot on my floor always warm or slightly damp?

That pattern usually means a hot water line is leaking directly under the slab in that area. It's one of the clearest signs of a slab leak, and it's worth having checked before it spreads further.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Once a slab leak is located, there are generally two paths: a direct repair. That means opening a small, targeted section of the slab to access and fix the pipe. The other path is a reroute, which runs a new line above the slab (typically through the attic or along a wall) and abandons the damaged section in place. We'll walk through both options with actual cost and disruption trade-offs for your specific situation before you decide.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Florida's slab-on-grade construction is standard here, which means nearly every home has supply lines running directly under the concrete foundation with no crawlspace access. That construction style is exactly why electronic tracing before any demolition matters so much in this market.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Slab Leaks By Neighborhood

Slab-on-grade construction is standard in this area, so slab leaks are a citywide risk, but the pattern differs by neighborhood. In older districts such as El Cid and Grandview Heights, aging copper and galvanized lines are often the cause. Closer to the water along Flagler Drive and the lagoon, constantly wet ground can mask the warm spot and damp floor that normally give a slab leak away. We trace the line electronically from the surface so the opening in the floor stays small and exact.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

After a slab repair, we recommend monitoring the water bill for the next billing cycle to confirm it's returned to baseline. If the leak was rerouted rather than repaired in place, the abandoned section under the slab is left safely capped and doesn't require any further attention.

We confirm the repair or reroute resolved the leak by rechecking the meter before we leave, so you're not left waiting to find out through next month's bill whether the fix actually worked.

Codes And Standards
