Tankless Water Heater Service in West Palm Beach, FL

Written by the West Palm Beach 24/7 Plumbing team. Last updated October 2026.

Tankless units last longer than tank heaters, but mineral scale means they still need regular descaling to keep performing at full capacity. Skipping this maintenance is the most common reason a tankless unit starts delivering inconsistent temperatures years before it should fail. Many manufacturer warranties on tankless units actually require documented regular maintenance to stay valid, which makes this service more than just a performance nicety.

FAQs

Common Questions

How often should a tankless unit be descaled here?

Annually is the common manufacturer recommendation, and more often if you notice temperature swings sooner or your water leaves visible scale. Check your unit's manual for its exact interval.

Can I do this myself?

Some homeowners do a basic flush themselves, but a professional service also checks for scale-related wear you wouldn't see otherwise.

Will this fix an error code on my unit?

Many error codes clear once scale buildup is removed, though some point to a separate component issue we'd diagnose during the visit.

Does scale damage tankless units faster than tank units?

Scale affects both types, but a tankless heat exchanger has narrow passages, so buildup shows up as temperature swings and error codes sooner. That is why regular descaling matters for tankless units.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

A standard descaling service involves circulating a manufacturer-approved descaling solution through the heat exchanger using isolation valves (most tankless units have these built in specifically for this purpose), followed by a full flush to clear loosened mineral deposits. We also inspect the inlet water filter, a component that's easy to overlook but that restricts flow when it gets clogged with the same sediment that scales the heat exchanger.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Snowbird households that leave tankless units idle for months during the off-season often come back to find scale buildup has already affected performance, since the mineral deposits don't pause just because the water isn't running daily. Scheduling a descale right before a seasonal return, rather than after noticing a problem, is a pattern we see work well for these households.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Tankless Units Around West Palm Beach

Tankless units are popular in renovated historic homes in El Cid and Flamingo Park, where owners want to free up closet space, and in newer downtown condominiums. In both settings mineral scale can build on the heat exchanger, and a unit that sat idle while owners were away for the summer often needs descaling before the first cool morning of the season.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling, and we're happy to note your unit for a reminder service call around the same time next year. Skipping a cycle isn't an emergency, but two or more skipped cycles is when heat exchanger wear typically starts becoming noticeable.

We test flow rate and output temperature after descaling to confirm the service actually restored performance, rather than just completing the flush procedure and assuming it worked.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

If your tankless unit needs more than a routine descale, full water heater installation and replacement covers new-unit installation. Pairing a tankless system with water softener installation is one of the more effective ways to slow the scale buildup that drives most tankless service calls here.
