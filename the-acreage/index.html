What Makes The Acreage Different

Why The Acreage Plumbing Is Its Own Market

Wells And Septic, Not City Utilities

Most homes here rely on private wells and septic systems rather than municipal water and sewer. Plumbing problems often involve a pressure tank, a long supply line, or a septic tank that needs pumping.

Large Lots And Long Supply Runs

The community was laid out with one-acre lots, and many homes sit well back from the road. Long runs of buried pipe give leaks plenty of room to hide, so electronic tracing saves time and digging.

Flat Land And Drainage Swales

The Indian Trail Improvement District maintains drainage here, and homes rely on swales, ponds, canals, and raised pads to manage water. After heavy rain, saturated ground can slow a septic drain field, so we look at drainage when a system backs up.

Farther From Town

The Acreage is roughly eighteen miles northwest of West Palm Beach. We quote any travel charge before a technician is dispatched, and the emergency line works the same here as anywhere else we serve.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4

Call our 24/7 line, a The Acreage technician can be dispatched immediately, day or night.

Our Commitment

Serving The Acreage

The Acreage behaves differently from the cities around it. Instead of a municipal water main and a sewer lateral, most homes depend on a well, a pressure tank, and a septic system, and each of those has its own failure signs. Dropping pressure, sputtering faucets, slow drains, and soggy ground are all worth a call.

Large lots mean long pipe runs and long drive times, so we ask the right questions before dispatch. We want to know whether the home is on a well, where the tank is, and what the symptoms are, so the technician arrives with the right equipment. We quote the work before we start and explain what we find.
