Water Damage Restoration in West Palm Beach, FL

Water damage restoration is a different job than fixing the pipe that caused it. Once a leak or flood has soaked flooring, drywall, or subfloor, that water needs to be extracted, the area dried and monitored to prevent mold, and any damaged materials removed and rebuilt. This service covers that cleanup and structural restoration work.

FAQs

Common Questions

Is this the same as fixing the leak that caused the damage?

No. This service covers the cleanup and structural drying/restoration. The plumbing repair that stopped the leak is handled separately as its own service.

Will this help with an insurance claim?

We document moisture readings and photos throughout the job, which most insurers require to process a water damage claim.

How long does drying take?

Most residential jobs take three to five days of monitored drying, depending on how much water was absorbed.

Do you work with my insurance company directly?

We provide the documentation your insurer requires, though the claim itself is filed and managed by you or your agent.

My ceiling has a stain that keeps getting bigger, what should I do?

A growing stain usually means the source is still active or the area never fully dried. That's worth addressing quickly, both to find and fix the plumbing source and to dry the area before mold becomes a second problem.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Our restoration equipment includes commercial-grade extraction pumps, air movers, and dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage, along with moisture meters used throughout the job to track progress against dry-standard benchmarks rather than guessing when a space feels dry. This service covers extraction, structural drying, and removal and replacement of damaged materials like drywall, baseboards, and flooring. It does not include the plumbing repair that caused the damage in the first place; that's scoped and quoted as a separate job.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Hurricane season brings a real spike in water damage calls here, from wind-driven rain intrusion to storm-related plumbing failures while a property is unoccupied. We factor South Florida humidity into every drying plan, since mold risk accelerates faster here than in drier climates if a space isn't fully dried and monitored.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

After drying is complete, we recommend a follow-up moisture check about two weeks out, especially in humid South Florida conditions where trapped moisture in wall cavities can take longer to fully evaporate than the surface readings suggest. Keeping the area well-ventilated during that window helps prevent any residual moisture from turning into a mold issue.

Our restoration work is documented with before, during, and after photos and moisture readings, so you have a clear record of what was affected, what was done, and confirmation the area met dry-standard benchmarks before we closed the job.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

The plumbing repair that caused the flooding is handled separately, most often leak detection to confirm the source or water line repair if a supply line failed. If water is still actively spreading when you call, water mitigation is the emergency-response step that happens first.
