FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need this in addition to a water softener?

They solve different problems. A softener addresses hardness minerals, while filtration addresses taste, odor, and sediment. Some households want both.

Will a whole-home filter reduce my water pressure?

Not when it's properly sized for your home's flow rate, which is something we check before recommending a system.

Is a filter under the kitchen sink enough?

If your only concern is drinking water taste, yes. If you want it at every tap, a whole-home system is the better fit.

Does a whole-home filter need regular filter changes?

Yes, most systems need filter cartridge changes on a schedule we'll walk you through at installation.

Why does my tap water taste like chlorine sometimes?

That's usually tied to seasonal treatment adjustments at the municipal level, and a whole-home carbon filtration system is the most common fix if it's a persistent, ongoing issue rather than an occasional one.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Whole-home systems are typically installed at the main water line entry point, often paired with a sediment pre-filter stage ahead of a carbon filtration stage for taste and odor. Point-of-use systems are installed at a single fixture, usually the kitchen sink, for households only concerned with drinking water quality. We test your water first to identify what's actually present before recommending which filtration approach fits.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

West Palm Beach draws from a municipal system that some residents notice a chlorine taste or odor from, particularly right after seasonal treatment adjustments, which is one of the most common reasons homeowners here look into whole-home filtration. That seasonal pattern is worth mentioning when we assess your water, since a system sized for a brief adjustment period differs from one meant for a persistent year-round taste concern.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

Filter cartridges need periodic replacement on a schedule that depends on the system type and your household's water usage, and we'll set expectations for that schedule at installation. Skipping cartridge changes doesn't damage the system, but it does gradually reduce how effectively it's actually filtering your water.

We test water quality after installation to confirm the system is addressing the specific issue you called about, whether that's taste, odor, or sediment.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

If mineral hardness rather than taste or odor is the actual concern, water softener installation is the more targeted fix. Some households benefit from both a filtration system and a check of the water heater, since hardness and filtration both affect how that unit performs over time.
