Water Heater Installation & Replacement in West Palm Beach, FL

Most tank water heaters last 10-15 years, and South Florida's hard water tends to shorten that further. When repair no longer makes sense, we install tank or tankless replacements from all major brands, with the old unit hauled away as part of the job. Replacing proactively before a full failure also avoids the scenario of no hot water for days while waiting on a same-day slot during a high-demand period.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Installation includes removing and hauling away the old unit, connecting the new unit to existing water and gas or electrical lines, updating venting if code requires it for the new unit type, and a full system test before we consider the job complete. We install standard tank units, high-efficiency tank units, and tankless systems, and will walk through the real trade-offs of each for your household's actual hot water usage.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Many West Palm Beach homes, especially older ones near downtown and in seasonal snowbird properties left vacant for months at a time, run water heaters well past their rated life without anyone noticing until a failure happens while the home is occupied again. Checking a unit's age during any other plumbing visit is a simple way to catch this before it becomes an inconvenient surprise.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

New units perform best with an annual flush to manage sediment buildup from this area's water hardness, starting about a year after installation. We'll walk through the manufacturer's specific maintenance recommendations for your new unit before we leave, since tank and tankless models have different upkeep needs. We'll also register the manufacturer warranty on your behalf where that's an option, so the coverage is active from day one without extra paperwork on your end.

Every new installation is tested for proper venting, pressure, and temperature before we leave, and we'll walk you through the unit's manufacturer warranty coverage so you know exactly what that covers separately from our installation work.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

If you're deciding between sticking with a tank or switching to an on-demand system, tankless water heater service covers what tankless maintenance looks like. Once your new unit is installed, pairing it with water softener installation helps it last longer given this area's water hardness.
