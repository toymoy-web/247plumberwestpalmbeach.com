Emergency Water Heater Replacement In West Palm Beach

When a tank fails outright, waiting days for a replacement is not realistic. Call our 24/7 line and we dispatch a technician to confirm the unit is beyond repair and quote a tank or tankless replacement at a flat price before any work starts.

A ruptured tank can release a lot of water quickly, so shut off the cold water supply to the heater and the power or gas control first. Then call, and we will plan removal, installation, and venting around your home.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

Installation includes removing and hauling away the old unit, connecting the new unit to existing water and gas or electrical lines, and updating venting if code requires it for the new unit type. We finish with a full system test before we consider the job complete. We install standard tank units, high-efficiency tank units, and tankless systems, and will walk through the real trade-offs of each for your household's actual hot water usage.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Many West Palm Beach homes run water heaters well past their rated life without anyone noticing until a failure happens. This is most common in older homes near downtown and in seasonal snowbird properties left vacant for months at a time. Checking a unit's age during any other plumbing visit is a simple way to catch this before it becomes an inconvenient surprise.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Replacement Jobs By Building Type

Replacement jobs in West Palm Beach usually come down to access. A 1920s cottage in Grandview Heights or Mango Promenade may need updated venting or a different location for the new unit. A downtown condominium needs building approval, a drain pan, and a delivery path that works with the elevator. Knowing the building type ahead of time is how we quote a fixed price that holds on installation day.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

New units perform best with an annual flush to manage sediment buildup, starting about a year after installation. We'll walk through the manufacturer's specific maintenance recommendations for your new unit before we leave, since tank and tankless models have different upkeep needs. We'll also register the manufacturer warranty on your behalf where that's an option, so the coverage is active from day one without extra paperwork on your end.

Every new installation is tested for proper venting, pressure, and temperature before we leave, and we'll walk you through the unit's manufacturer warranty coverage so you know exactly what that covers separately from our installation work.

Codes And Standards

Work Done To Code

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), requires relief valve protection and a safe discharge on storage water heaters, and venting must match the unit type. We follow the manufacturer's installation guidelines for every model we install.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

If you're deciding between sticking with a tank or switching to an on-demand system, tankless water heater service covers what tankless maintenance looks like. Once your new unit is installed, pairing it with water softener installation can help it last longer if your water leaves heavy scale.
