FAQs

Common Questions

Why is my water pressure low throughout the whole house?

That pattern, rather than just one fixture, often points to the main service line itself rather than internal plumbing.

Can the line be replaced without digging up my whole yard?

In many cases, yes. Trenchless methods can pull a new line through with a much smaller access point.

Is this the same as the water meter itself failing?

No, a meter issue is on the utility's side. This service covers the line between the meter and your home.

Will my water be shut off during the repair?

Yes, temporarily, though we schedule the shutoff window to minimize how long you're without water.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

For repairs, we isolate the damaged section using the main shutoff and, where possible, complete a spot repair without a full line replacement. For full replacements, trenchless methods can pull a new line through the existing path using a smaller access pit rather than trenching the entire yard. Material options include copper and modern PEX-rated pipe, both of which outperform the original galvanized material common in older service lines here. Work of this scope is permitted and inspected under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

Many original West Palm Beach neighborhoods still have galvanized service lines running from the street, and the combination of that aging material with the area's water chemistry is a common, real driver of the corrosion and pressure loss we're called out for.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

After a repair or replacement, water pressure and flow are confirmed at every fixture before we consider the job done, and there's generally no follow-up maintenance needed for the new line itself. If only a section was repaired rather than the full line, it's worth knowing the material and age of the remaining sections in case another spot needs attention down the road.

We confirm pressure and flow at every fixture in the house after the work is complete, not just at the point of repair, since a line issue can sometimes affect pressure throughout the home.

Related Services

Often Booked Together

A failing service line is sometimes found during a general leak detection visit rather than reported directly, since low pressure and high bills look similar to other leak symptoms. If the line's condition points to a pattern of aging pipe throughout the home, that can lead into a repiping conversation.
