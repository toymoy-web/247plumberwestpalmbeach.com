FAQs

Common Questions

Is water softener installation really worth it in West Palm Beach?

It depends on your water. If a test shows heavy scale-forming minerals, a softener can reduce deposits on fixtures and sediment in your water heater. If the numbers are low, we will tell you it is not worth the cost.

My softener stopped regenerating, does it need replacing?

Not necessarily. Valve and resin bed issues are common repairs that often restore full function without a new system.

How is the right system size determined?

We test your actual water hardness and factor in household size and usage, rather than defaulting to a standard size.

Will a softener affect my drinking water taste?

Some households add a separate drinking water filter alongside a softener if taste is a specific concern, and we can advise on that combination.

Scope Of Service

What's Included

We test your actual water hardness in grains per gallon rather than assuming a standard local number, since hardness can vary somewhat by neighborhood and source. Sizing accounts for household size, number of bathrooms, and daily water usage. For existing systems, common repairs include resin bed replacement, control valve service, and brine tank cleaning, all of which can restore a softener that's stopped regenerating properly without a full system replacement.

Local Notes

Serving West Palm Beach & Palm Beach County

West Palm Beach treats surface water drawn from the Grassy Waters Preserve and also buys finished water from Palm Beach County, so the water at your tap can differ by address. Hardness is not published as one city-wide figure we can quote, which is why we test your tap rather than assume.

West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Scale Across West Palm Beach Neighborhoods

Mineral scale shows up differently depending on the home. In historic districts like El Cid and Flamingo Park, scale builds inside decades-old supply piping and narrows it over time, while newer condominiums see it first on fixtures and in water heaters. A softener at the main line protects both, and the right size depends on whether the home is a small cottage near Howard Park or a multi-bath house on the lagoon.

After The Job

What To Expect Next

New and repaired systems need periodic salt refills in the brine tank, which we'll walk through based on your specific unit's regeneration cycle and your household's water usage. Most systems also benefit from an annual resin bed check to confirm the system is still performing at full capacity.

After installation or repair, we retest your water hardness to confirm the system is actually performing to the level it was sized for, not just assume it will based on the equipment specs.

Codes And Standards

Work Done To Code

Residential salt-based softeners are certified under NSF/ANSI 44, the standard for cation exchange water softeners. Check for that certification on any unit you are quoted, and ask which hardness test result the sizing is based on. Installation follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Related Services

Often Booked Together

A softener addresses hardness minerals specifically. If taste, odor, or sediment is the actual concern instead, water filtration installation is the better fit. Because sediment is a common cause of early water heater wear, pairing a softener with regular water heater service helps protect that investment.
