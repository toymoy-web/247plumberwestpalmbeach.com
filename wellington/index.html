What Makes Wellington Different

Why Wellington Plumbing Is Its Own Market

Equestrian Properties And Barns

Horse properties often add barn water lines, wash racks, hose bibs, and large irrigation zones to the usual house plumbing. Long outdoor supply runs give leaks plenty of room to hide.

Drainage And A High Water Table

Because the village was built on land drained for farming, groundwater sits close to the surface in many areas. Buried leaks and sewer problems are harder to read by eye, which is why electronic tracing and cameras matter here.

Seasonal Occupancy

Some homes, especially rentals near the show grounds, sit empty for part of the year and then run at full demand from January through March. A slow leak that went unnoticed all summer shows up as a high bill when the season starts.

Very Different Communities

Gated communities such as The Isles at Wellington and VillageWalk, large-lot estates like Palm Beach Point with its five-acre minimum lots, and newer subdivisions each have different plumbing layouts and access rules.

Village Overview

Plumbing In The Village Of Wellington

Coverage

Wellington is a village of about 61,600 residents roughly fifteen miles west of West Palm Beach. It incorporated on December 31, 1995, and is now the fifth largest municipality in Palm Beach County.

The land was drained for farming under a drainage district created in 1953. Today the village is best known for the Winter Equestrian Festival, the Palm Beach International Equestrian Center, the International Polo Club Palm Beach, and The Mall at Wellington Green. Winter brings a surge of seasonal residents and horse-industry visitors, so plumbing demand here follows the equestrian calendar as much as the weather.

What To Do Before We Arrive

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak

1

Shut off the main water valve, or the nearest fixture valve if the leak is contained to one area.

2

If water is near outlets, a panel, or plugged-in appliances, cut power to that area at the breaker if it's safe to reach.

3

Clear the immediate area, move anything that can be moved away from standing water.

4

Call our 24/7 line, a Wellington technician can be dispatched immediately, day or night.
